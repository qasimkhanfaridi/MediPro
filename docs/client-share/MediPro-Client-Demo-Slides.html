The idea

Order capture for licensed pharmacies

For pharmacies

Browse your catalogue, see stock availability, add to cart, submit orders from phone or desktop.

For your ops team

Approve stores, manage products & offers, filter orders by city/area, get alerts when work arrives.

What it is not (yet)

Not a billing/ERP replacement. Not online payments. Not a patient-facing pharmacy app.
